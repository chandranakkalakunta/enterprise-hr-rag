# Remote Work Policy - TechCorp India

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 26, 2023
**Review Date:** October 26, 2024

---

## 1. Introduction

TechCorp India recognizes the importance of flexibility, employee well-being, and maintaining high productivity in a dynamic work environment. This Remote Work Policy outlines the guidelines and expectations for employees working remotely (Work From Home - WFH) on a regular, hybrid basis. This policy aims to ensure fairness, consistency, and continued operational excellence while offering employees the flexibility to balance work and personal commitments.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:
*   Define eligibility criteria for remote work.
*   Establish clear expectations for remote work schedules and productivity.
*   Outline responsibilities for equipment, internet connectivity, and data security.
*   Ensure clear communication and collaboration between remote and in-office team members.
*   Maintain a safe and productive work environment, regardless of location.
*   Support business continuity and employee satisfaction.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to all eligible full-time employees of TechCorp India. It covers regularly scheduled remote work arrangements and does not cover temporary or ad-hoc remote work due to emergencies (e.g., natural disaster, severe weather), which will be managed on a case-by-case basis.

## 4. Definitions

*   **Remote Work (WFH):** Performing work duties from a location other than the TechCorp India office, typically the employee's designated home office.
*   **Hybrid Work Model:** A work model where employees split their time between working from the office and working remotely.
*   **Core Hours:** Specific hours during the workday when all employees are expected to be available and responsive for collaboration and meetings.
*   **Designated Home Office:** The primary location (e.g., employee's residence within India) from which an employee regularly performs remote work.

## 5. Eligibility for Remote Work

To be eligible for remote work, an employee must meet the following criteria:

*   **Role Suitability:** The employee's role must be identified by their department head and HR as suitable for remote work, meaning it does not require constant physical presence in the office (e.g., lab work, specific hardware development, client-facing roles requiring physical presence).
*   **Performance:** The employee must consistently meet or exceed performance expectations as evidenced by recent performance reviews and ongoing performance management. Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are generally not eligible.
*   **Tenure:** Employees must have completed at least **six (6) months** of continuous service with TechCorp India.
*   **Manager Approval:** Remote work arrangements require explicit approval from the employee's direct manager and the respective Department Head.
*   **Technical Setup:** The employee must have a suitable, secure, and ergonomically sound workspace at their designated home office within India.

## 6. Remote Work Schedule - 2 Days WFH Per Week

TechCorp India operates on a hybrid model with the following guidelines:

*   **Standard Schedule:** Eligible employees are permitted to work remotely for **up to two (2) days per standard five-day work week**.
*   **In-Office Requirement:** Employees are required to work from the TechCorp India office for a minimum of **three (3) days per standard five-day work week**.
*   **Day Selection:** The specific remote work days will be determined in consultation with the direct manager and team, taking into account team collaboration needs, project requirements, and departmental schedules. Managers have the discretion to approve or adjust remote work days to ensure operational efficiency.
*   **Flexibility:** While the standard is 2 WFH days, managers may, in exceptional circumstances and with prior approval, request an employee to come to the office on a scheduled WFH day, or grant additional WFH days, especially for critical project deadlines or personal emergencies.
*   **No Accumulation:** Unused remote work days cannot be accumulated or carried over to subsequent weeks.

## 7. Core Working Hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM IST)

*   **Availability:** All employees, whether working remotely or from the office, are required to be online, available, and responsive during the core hours of **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM Indian Standard Time (IST)**.
*   **Collaboration:** This period is designated for critical team meetings, collaborative tasks, and urgent communications, ensuring seamless interaction across the organization.
*   **Workday Completion:** Employees are expected to complete their full contractual working hours (typically 8-9 hours per day, including a lunch break) outside of these core hours, as per their team's and project's requirements. Flexibility is encouraged around the core hours, provided daily and weekly work expectations are met.

## 8. Equipment Policy

*   **Company-Provided Equipment:** TechCorp India will provide a standard company laptop, monitor (if applicable as per standard office setup), keyboard, and mouse for employees approved for remote work. This equipment remains the property of TechCorp India and must be used primarily for official business.
*   **Maintenance & Support:** IT support will be provided for company-issued equipment. Employees are responsible for promptly reporting any issues with company equipment.
*   **Personal Equipment:** While employees may choose to use certain personal peripheral devices (e.g., headphones, webcam), TechCorp India will not be responsible for the maintenance, repair, or security of personal equipment used for work purposes.
*   **Return of Equipment:** Upon termination of employment or cessation of remote work arrangement, all company-provided equipment must be returned to TechCorp India in good working condition.

## 9. Internet Reimbursement Policy

*   **Reimbursement Amount:** Eligible employees approved for remote work will receive a monthly internet reimbursement of up to **INR 750 (Rupees Seven Hundred and Fifty Only)** to cover the cost of their home internet connection.
*   **Eligibility:** Reimbursement is applicable only for months during which the employee has an active and approved remote work arrangement.
*   **Claim Process:**
    *   Employees must submit a valid proof of internet connection and monthly bill/receipt.
    *   Claims should be submitted through the designated HR/Finance portal by the specified deadline each month.
    *   Reimbursement will be processed along with the monthly salary, subject to verification.
*   **Quality Expectation:** Employees are responsible for ensuring they have a reliable, high-speed internet connection sufficient for performing their job duties, including video conferencing and accessing company systems. The company is not liable for internet service disruptions at the employee's home.

## 10. Attendance Tracking and Management

*   **Daily Check-in/Check-out:** All employees, including those working remotely, are required to log their daily attendance using the company's designated HR Information System (HRIS) or attendance management tool. This includes logging in at the start of their workday and logging out at the end.
*   **Availability Status:** Employees are expected to maintain an active status on company communication platforms (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Slack) during their working hours, especially during core hours.
*   **Manager Oversight:** Managers are responsible for monitoring their team's attendance, productivity, and adherence to working hours. Any discrepancies or concerns regarding attendance or availability should be addressed promptly with the employee and, if necessary, escalated to HR.
*   **Compliance:** Consistent failure to log attendance or maintain availability during designated working hours may lead to disciplinary action.

## 11. Communication and Collaboration

*   **Responsiveness:** Employees working remotely are expected to be as responsive and accessible as their in-office counterparts. Prompt responses to emails, messages, and calls are essential.
*   **Communication Tools:** Employees must actively use designated company communication and collaboration tools (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Outlook, Jira, Confluence) for all work-related interactions.
*   **Video Conferencing:** All team and project meetings will be conducted via video conferencing, and remote employees are expected to join with video enabled whenever possible to foster engagement and connection.
*   **Proactive Updates:** Remote employees are encouraged to proactively communicate their progress, challenges, and availability to their managers and team members.

## 12. Data Security and Confidentiality

*   **Secure Environment:** Employees must ensure their designated home office provides a secure and private environment for handling confidential company information.
*   **Network Security:** All work must be conducted over secure, password-protected networks. Use of public Wi-Fi networks for company work is strictly prohibited. Employees must connect to the company's Virtual Private Network (VPN) as required by IT policies.
*   **Confidentiality:** Employees must adhere to all TechCorp India's data security, privacy, and confidentiality policies, including protecting company data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
*   **Physical Security:** Company equipment and confidential documents must be kept secure and out of sight from unauthorized individuals.
*   **Reporting Incidents:** Any suspected data breach, security incident, or loss of company equipment must be reported immediately to the IT Department.

## 13. Health, Safety, and Well-being

*   **Ergonomics:** Employees are responsible for setting up an ergonomically sound and safe workspace at home to prevent injuries and maintain well-being. TechCorp India provides resources and guidance on ergonomic best practices.
*   **Breaks:** Employees are encouraged to take regular breaks, including a dedicated lunch break, to prevent fatigue and maintain focus.
*   **Work-Life Balance:** Remote employees are encouraged to maintain a healthy work-life balance and disconnect from work outside of their designated working hours.
*   **Reporting Incidents:** Any work-related injuries or incidents occurring during remote work hours must be reported immediately to the manager and HR.

## 14. Performance Management

*   Performance expectations for remote employees are the same as for in-office employees. Managers will focus on measurable outcomes, quality of work, and contributions rather than just hours spent.
*   Regular check-ins, performance reviews, and feedback sessions will continue as per company policy.

## 15. Manager Responsibilities

*   Approve and manage remote work schedules for their team.
*   Ensure clear communication channels and collaboration within the team.
*   Monitor team performance, productivity, and well-being.
*   Provide necessary support and resources for remote team members.
*   Ensure compliance with this policy.

## 16. Employee Responsibilities

*   Adhere to all aspects of this Remote Work Policy and all other company policies.
*   Maintain productivity and high-quality work output.
*   Ensure a suitable, safe, and secure remote work environment.
*   Maintain clear and proactive communication with managers and team members.
*   Be available and responsive during core hours and complete full working hours.
*   Protect company assets and information.

## 17. Policy Review and Amendments

This policy will be reviewed annually or as needed to ensure its continued effectiveness and relevance. TechCorp India reserves the right to amend or revoke this policy at its sole discretion, with prior notice to employees.

## 18. Non-Compliance and Violations

Failure to comply with any part of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, as per TechCorp India's Disciplinary Policy. Repeated or severe non-compliance may also lead to the revocation of remote work privileges.
