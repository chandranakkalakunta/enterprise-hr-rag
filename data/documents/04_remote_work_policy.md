This comprehensive Remote Work Policy outlines the framework for employees of ChandraAILabs, an AI/ML product company based in Hyderabad, India, to work remotely. It aims to provide flexibility while maintaining productivity, collaboration, and the company culture.

---

# ChandraAILabs Remote Work Policy

**Policy Number:** CIL-HR-003
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 26th October 2023
**Policy Owner:** HR Department

---

## 1. Introduction

ChandraAILabs believes in fostering a flexible and results-oriented work environment. This policy outlines the guidelines for remote work arrangements, balancing employee autonomy with organizational needs for collaboration, security, and sustained innovation. We aim to leverage the benefits of remote work to attract and retain top talent, enhance employee well-being, and ensure business continuity.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees of ChandraAILabs based in Hyderabad, India, unless explicitly stated otherwise for specific roles or teams. Contractors, interns, and part-time employees are generally excluded, though exceptions may be made at the discretion of their reporting manager and HR.

## 3. Definitions

*   **Remote Work:** Performing work duties from a location other than the ChandraAILabs office (e.g., home, co-working space) for all or part of the work week.
*   **Hybrid Work:** A model where employees split their work time between the ChandraAILabs office and a remote location.
*   **Full Remote Work:** A model where employees work entirely from a remote location, with occasional, mandatory visits to the office for specific events.
*   **Core Hours:** Designated hours during which all employees are expected to be available for communication and collaboration.
*   **WFH:** Work From Home.

## 4. General Principles

*   **Trust & Accountability:** We trust our employees to manage their time effectively and be accountable for their deliverables.
*   **Flexibility & Collaboration:** The policy aims to provide flexibility while ensuring strong team collaboration and maintaining our vibrant company culture.
*   **Productivity & Performance:** Remote work should not compromise productivity or performance standards.
*   **Security & Compliance:** All remote work must adhere to ChandraAILabs' data security, confidentiality, and compliance policies.
*   **Equality:** All employees, whether remote or in-office, will be treated equally regarding performance evaluations, opportunities for advancement, and access to company resources.

## 5. Eligibility for Remote Work

Eligibility for remote work is determined based on the following criteria:

*   **Role Suitability:** The employee's role and responsibilities must be conducive to remote work, as determined by their manager and department head. Roles requiring constant physical presence, access to specialized on-site equipment, or handling highly sensitive physical materials may not be eligible.
*   **Performance:** The employee must be in good standing with the company, demonstrating consistent strong performance and adherence to company policies.
*   **Tenure:** Employees must have successfully completed their probationary period (typically 3-6 months).
*   **Home Work Environment:** The employee must have a suitable, safe, and ergonomically sound remote workspace with reliable internet connectivity and minimal distractions.
*   **Manager Approval:** All remote work arrangements require explicit approval from the employee's direct manager and department head.

## 6. Remote Work Models

ChandraAILabs operates primarily on a hybrid model, with a specific full-remote provision for AI teams.

### 6.1. Hybrid Work Model (Default for most teams)

*   **Office Attendance:** Employees under the hybrid model are required to work from the ChandraAILabs office for a minimum of **2 days per week**.
*   **Remote Work Days:** Employees can work remotely for **up to 3 days per week**.
*   **Scheduling:** The specific office days will be determined by individual teams and managers to optimize collaboration and team cohesion. Teams are encouraged to designate common in-office days.
*   **Purpose of Office Days:** Office days are crucial for face-to-face collaboration, team meetings, brainstorming sessions, mentorship, team building, and maintaining company culture.

### 6.2. Full Remote Work Model (Specific to AI Teams)

*   **Eligibility:** This model is specifically for employees within **AI/ML Engineering, AI Research, and Data Science teams**.
*   **Location:** Employees in these eligible teams may work fully remotely from **any location within India**. International remote work is not permitted under this policy.
*   **Office Visits:** Full remote employees are required to travel to the Hyderabad office for mandatory company-wide events (e.g., quarterly town halls, annual strategy meetings, team-specific workshops) as deemed necessary by the company. Travel and accommodation for such mandatory visits will be reimbursed by ChandraAILabs as per the company's travel policy.
*   **Manager Discretion:** While full remote is the default for eligible AI teams, managers retain the discretion to require occasional in-office presence for critical project milestones, intensive collaboration, or team-building activities, with reasonable notice.

## 7. Work Hours and Attendance

*   **Core Hours:** All employees (hybrid and full remote) are expected to be available and responsive during **Core Hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time)**, Monday to Friday.
*   **Flexibility:** While core hours are mandatory, employees have the flexibility to complete their remaining daily work hours (typically 8-9 hours per day as per employment contract) outside these core hours, provided it doesn't negatively impact team collaboration or deliverables.
*   **Attendance Tracking:**
    *   Employees are required to log their attendance daily via the designated HRMS system (e.g., Punch-in/Punch-out).
    *   Managers will monitor attendance and productivity based on deliverables, engagement, and availability during core hours.
    *   Any planned unavailability during core hours (e.g., doctor's appointment) must be communicated in advance to the manager and team.
*   **Breaks:** Employees are entitled to regular breaks during their workday.

## 8. Equipment and Internet Policy

### 8.1. Company-Provided Equipment

*   ChandraAILabs will provide the necessary primary work equipment, including a laptop, external monitor, keyboard, and mouse.
*   Employees are responsible for the proper care and maintenance of company-issued equipment.
*   Any damage, loss, or malfunction must be reported immediately to IT Support.
*   Upon termination of employment, all company-issued equipment must be returned promptly in good working condition.

### 8.2. Personal Equipment

*   Employees are generally discouraged from using personal devices for company work due to security and compliance reasons. Exceptions may be made with explicit IT approval and adherence to specific security protocols.
*   ChandraAILabs will not be responsible for the maintenance, repair, or security of personal equipment used for work purposes.

### 8.3. Internet Reimbursement

*   To support remote work, ChandraAILabs will provide a **monthly internet allowance of INR 1000/-** to all eligible remote and hybrid employees.
*   Employees must maintain a reliable high-speed internet connection suitable for business operations (video conferencing, data transfer).
*   Reimbursement will be processed monthly upon submission of proof of active internet service (e.g., bill copy).

### 8.4. Work Environment & Safety

*   Employees are responsible for establishing a safe, private, and ergonomically sound workspace at their remote location.
*   ChandraAILabs is not responsible for injuries or accidents occurring in an employee's remote workspace unless directly caused by company-provided equipment or instructions.
*   Employees should ensure they have adequate power backup solutions (e.g., UPS for critical equipment) to maintain continuity during power outages common in India.

## 9. Communication and Collaboration

*   **Tools:** All employees are expected to utilize company-approved communication and collaboration tools (e.g., Slack, Google Meet, Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Teams) effectively.
*   **Responsiveness:** Employees must be responsive to communications during core hours.
*   **Virtual Meetings:** Regular virtual team meetings, one-on-ones, and project discussions are encouraged to maintain cohesion and progress. Video should be enabled during meetings where possible.
*   **Transparency:** Managers will ensure that remote employees are included in all relevant communications, meetings, and decision-making processes.

## 10. Data Security and Confidentiality

*   **VPN:** Employees must use a company-provided VPN for accessing ChandraAILabs' internal networks and sensitive data.
*   **Secure Networks:** Work should only be performed on secure, password-protected networks. Public Wi-Fi networks are strictly prohibited for company work.
*   **Device Security:** Laptops and other devices must be kept secure, password-protected, and adhere to all IT security policies (e.g., regular software updates, antivirus protection).
*   **Data Handling:** Company data must not be stored on personal devices or unsecured cloud services. Employees must follow all data handling and confidentiality protocols.
*   **Privacy:** Employees must ensure their remote workspace maintains confidentiality, preventing unauthorized individuals from viewing or accessing company information.

## 11. Performance Management

*   Performance expectations and evaluation criteria remain the same for both remote and in-office employees.
*   Managers will focus on deliverables, outcomes, and adherence to timelines rather than physical presence.
*   Regular check-ins and performance reviews will be conducted as per the existing performance management framework.

## 12. Expense Reimbursement (Other)

*   Beyond the internet allowance, any other work-related expenses incurred by remote employees must be pre-approved by their manager and HR.
*   Employees are responsible for expenses related to their remote work setup (e.g., furniture, utilities, personal internet plan exceeding the allowance) unless otherwise agreed upon.

## 13. Policy Review and Amendments

This policy will be reviewed periodically (at least annually) by the HR Department and management to ensure its effectiveness and relevance. ChandraAILabs reserves the right to amend, modify, or revoke this policy at any time, with reasonable notice to employees.

## 14. Policy Violations

Failure to adhere to the terms of this Remote Work Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, in accordance with ChandraAILabs' disciplinary policies.
