# ChandraAILabs Performance Management Policy

---

## 1. Introduction

ChandraAILabs is committed to fostering a culture of high performance, continuous growth, and innovation. This Performance Management Policy outlines the framework for setting clear expectations, providing regular feedback, recognizing achievements, and addressing performance gaps. Our goal is to align individual and team efforts with ChandraAILabs' strategic objectives, drive the success of our AI/ML products, and support the professional development of every employee.

This policy applies to all full-time employees of ChandraAILabs, effective from [Date].

## 2. Policy Objectives

*   To align individual and team goals with ChandraAILabs' overall strategic objectives and AI product vision.
*   To provide a fair, transparent, and objective system for evaluating employee performance.
*   To encourage continuous feedback, coaching, and open communication between managers and employees.
*   To identify and nurture talent, facilitate professional development, and support career progression.
*   To recognize and reward high performance, particularly as it contributes to our AI product success.
*   To address performance deficiencies constructively and provide support for improvement.
*   To maintain a high-performance culture that drives innovation and excellence in AI/ML.

## 3. Key Principles

*   **Transparency:** Employees will have a clear understanding of expectations, evaluation criteria, and the performance management process.
*   **Fairness & Objectivity:** Assessments will be based on observable behaviors, measurable results, and established criteria, free from bias.
*   **Continuous Development:** Performance management is an ongoing process focused on growth, learning, and skill enhancement.
*   **Accountability:** Employees and managers are mutually accountable for performance and development.
*   **Alignment:** Individual and team efforts are directly linked to the company's AI product roadmap and business success.
*   **Data-Driven:** Where possible, performance will be assessed using quantitative and qualitative data relevant to AI/ML development and product impact.

## 4. Goal Setting: Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)

ChandraAILabs utilizes the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework for goal setting.

### 4.1. OKR Definition

*   **Objectives (O):** Ambitious, qualitative, time-bound, and actionable goals that state *what* is to be achieved. They should be inspiring and challenging.
*   **Key Results (KR):** Measurable, quantitative metrics that define *how* progress towards an Objective will be measured. They should be specific, trackable, and verifiable. KRs are typically measured on a 0-1.0 scale (or percentage).

### 4.2. OKR Cadence

*   **Annual Company OKRs:** Set by senior leadership, defining the strategic direction for the year.
*   **Quarterly Department/Team OKRs:** Cascaded from company OKRs, defining specific goals for each team.
*   **Quarterly Individual OKRs:** Developed by employees in collaboration with their managers, aligning with team and company OKRs.
*   **Review & Adjustment:** OKRs are reviewed monthly/bi-monthly in 1:1s and formally assessed quarterly. Adjustments can be made mid-quarter if business priorities shift significantly.

### 4.3. Link to AI Product Success

Individual and team OKRs will directly contribute to the development, improvement, and success of ChandraAILabs' AI/ML products. Examples include:

*   **Objective:** Enhance the accuracy and reliability of our core AI model.
    *   **Key Result:** Reduce inference error rate by 15% in Model X.
    *   **Key Result:** Achieve an F1-score of 0.92 for sentiment analysis feature Y.
*   **Objective:** Accelerate the deployment of new AI features.
    *   **Key Result:** Launch new feature Z to production with A/B testing completed within Q2.
    *   **Key Result:** Decrease average model deployment time from 2 weeks to 3 days.
*   **Objective:** Improve AI product user experience and adoption.
    *   **Key Result:** Increase daily active users of AI dashboard by 20%.
    *   **Key Result:** Achieve a customer satisfaction score (CSAT) of 4.5/5.0 related to AI features.

## 5. Performance Review Cycle

ChandraAILabs conducts a structured performance review process to assess employee contributions, progress on OKRs, and overall professional development.

### 5.1. Mid-Year Performance Review (July)

*   **Purpose:** A formal check-in to review progress against established OKRs and development plans. It's an opportunity for employees and managers to discuss challenges, provide constructive feedback, and make necessary adjustments to goals or work priorities for the remainder of the year.
*   **Process:**
    1.  **Self-Assessment:** Employee submits a self-assessment reflecting on their performance, achievements, and challenges during the first half of the year.
    2.  **Manager Assessment:** Manager reviews the employee's performance, OKR progress, and provides feedback.
    3.  **One-on-One Discussion:** Manager and employee have a dedicated meeting to discuss performance, feedback, and development areas.
    4.  **No Formal Rating:** A formal performance rating is typically *not* assigned during the mid-year review. The focus is on alignment and course correction.
    5.  **Documentation:** Key discussion points, feedback, and any adjusted goals/development plans are documented.

### 5.2. Annual Performance Review (January-February)

*   **Purpose:** A comprehensive evaluation of an employee's performance over the entire calendar year. It encompasses OKR achievement, contribution to team and company goals, demonstration of core competencies, and adherence to company values. This review forms the basis for performance ratings, promotion considerations, and compensation adjustments.
*   **Process:**
    1.  **Self-Assessment (December):** Employee reflects on their annual achievements, OKR attainment, challenges, and development areas, providing evidence and context.
    2.  **Manager Assessment (January):** Manager evaluates the employee's performance against OKRs, job description, competencies, and company values. This includes considering peer feedback (if applicable) and other relevant data.
    3.  **Calibration Meeting (January):** Managers meet with their leadership and HR to review and calibrate ratings across departments/teams to ensure consistency and fairness.
    4.  **Performance Discussion (February):** Manager conducts a detailed one-on-one meeting with the employee to discuss the comprehensive review, provide specific feedback, assign a formal performance rating, and discuss a development plan for the upcoming year.
    5.  **Employee Acknowledgment:** Employee acknowledges receipt and discussion of the review (via signature or digital confirmation). They may add comments or a rebuttal if they disagree with the assessment.
    6.  **Finalization:** The completed review form is submitted to HR for record-keeping.

## 6. Performance Rating Scale (1-5)

ChandraAILabs uses a 5-point rating scale to evaluate employee performance during the Annual Performance Review.

*   **5 - Outstanding / Exceeds All Expectations:**
    *   Consistently performs far above job requirements and expectations.
    *   Achieves all OKRs and significantly exceeds several, often delivering results beyond the initial scope.
    *   Demonstrates exceptional initiative, innovation, and problem-solving skills, particularly in complex AI/ML challenges.
    *   Serves as a role model for peers, proactively mentors others, and contributes significantly to team and company success.
    *   Has a profound positive impact on AI product development, quality, or strategic direction.

*   **4 - Exceeds Most Expectations:**
    *   Consistently performs above job requirements and expectations in most areas.
    *   Achieves all core OKRs and exceeds several, demonstrating high quality and efficiency.
    *   Takes initiative, delivers high-quality work, and handles complex assignments effectively.
    *   A strong contributor to team goals and AI product initiatives.
    *   Often takes on additional responsibilities and shows strong potential for growth.

*   **3 - Meets All Expectations:**
    *   Consistently meets all job requirements and expectations.
    *   Achieves all primary OKRs, delivering solid and reliable results.
    *   Demonstrates required skills, knowledge, and competencies for the role.
    *   A reliable and effective team member who contributes consistently to AI product development and team objectives.
    *   Successfully completes assigned tasks and projects on time and to standard. *This is the expected level of performance for all employees.*

*   **2 - Needs Improvement / Partially Meets Expectations:**
    *   Performance partially meets expectations and falls short in some key areas.
    *   Achieves some, but not all, OKRs, or achieves them with significant delays or quality issues.
    *   Requires consistent guidance and support to meet job requirements.
    *   May demonstrate inconsistencies in quality, productivity, or adherence to standards, impacting AI product timelines or quality.
    *   Requires a structured plan for improvement (e.g., a Performance Improvement Plan - PIP).

*   **1 - Does Not Meet Expectations:**
    *   Performance consistently falls significantly below job requirements and expectations.
    *   Fails to achieve critical OKRs despite support and guidance.
    *   Lacks essential skills or knowledge for the role, or consistently demonstrates behaviors that negatively impact team or AI product goals.
    *   Significant and persistent performance deficiencies that require immediate and serious intervention.
    *   May lead to a formal PIP or further disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 7. Continuous Feedback and Coaching

Performance management is an ongoing process. Managers are expected to:

*   Conduct regular one-on-one meetings (e.g., weekly or bi-weekly) with their team members.
*   Provide timely, specific, and actionable feedback (both positive and constructive).
*   Act as coaches, supporting employees in their development and problem-solving.
*   Recognize and appreciate good performance informally and formally.
*   Address performance concerns promptly and directly.

Employees are expected to actively seek feedback, communicate challenges, and take ownership of their development.

## 8. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is a formal process designed to address significant and consistent performance deficiencies.

### 8.1. Triggers for a PIP

*   A formal performance rating of '1' or '2' during the Annual Review.
*   Consistent failure to meet job expectations or critical OKRs, despite informal feedback and coaching.
*   Breach of company policies or professional conduct standards that impacts performance.

### 8.2. PIP Process

1.  **Identification:** Manager identifies a performance gap that requires formal intervention.
2.  **Initial Discussion & Warning:** Manager meets with the employee (and HR representative if deemed necessary) to discuss specific performance concerns, provide clear examples, explain the seriousness of the issue, and warn of potential consequences.
3.  **PIP Development:** HR, the manager, and the employee collaborate to create a written PIP. The PIP will clearly outline:
    *   **Specific Areas for Improvement:** Detailing the exact behaviors, skills, or results that need to change.
    *   **Clear, Measurable Goals/OKRs:** What successful performance looks like, with quantifiable targets. (e.g., "Improve model accuracy from X% to Y% on dataset Z," "Complete assigned code reviews within 24 hours 95% of the time").
    *   **Action Plan:** Steps the employee must take to achieve the goals.
    *   **Support & Resources:** Training, coaching, mentoring, or tools ChandraAILabs will provide.
    *   **Timeline:** A reasonable period for improvement (typically 30, 60, or 90 days, depending on the severity and nature of the issue).
    *   **Check-in Schedule:** Regular meetings (e.g., weekly) to monitor progress.
    *   **Consequences of Non-Improvement:** Clearly state the potential outcomes if performance does not improve to the required standard, which may include further disciplinary action, demotion, or termination of employment.
4.  **PIP Implementation & Monitoring:** Regular check-ins occur as scheduled. Manager provides continuous feedback and support, and documents progress.
5.  **PIP Review & Outcome:** At the end of the PIP period, a final review meeting is held. Possible outcomes include:
    *   **Successful Completion:** Performance has improved to a satisfactory level. Employee exits the PIP.
    *   **Partial Improvement:** Performance has improved but not consistently to the required level. The PIP may be extended, or other actions considered.
    *   **Non-Completion:** Performance has not improved, or has regressed. This may lead to further disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

## 9. Promotion Criteria

ChandraAILabs is committed to fostering career growth. Promotions are based on a combination of sustained high performance, demonstrated readiness for increased responsibility, and alignment with business needs.

### 9.1. Eligibility Criteria

*   **Sustained High Performance:** Consistently achieving a rating of '4' or '5' in Annual Reviews over the past 1-2 years.
*   **Demonstrated Competencies:** Clearly exhibiting the skills, knowledge, and behaviors required for the next level/role, including leadership, strategic thinking, mentorship, and impact beyond current responsibilities.
*   **Significant Contribution to AI Products:** Proven ability to drive innovation, solve complex AI/ML challenges, or significantly enhance ChandraAILabs' product offerings.
*   **Readiness for Increased Responsibility:** Taking initiative, mentoring junior colleagues, leading projects, or proactively seeking opportunities for growth.
*   **Business Need:** An open position or a strategic requirement for a higher-level role within the organization.

### 9.2. Promotion Process

1.  **Nomination:** Manager, HR, or self-nomination (with manager support) can initiate a promotion discussion.
2.  **Review of Qualifications:** Manager (in consultation with HR) assesses the employee's qualifications, performance history, and readiness against the criteria for the target role.
3.  **Interview/Presentation (if applicable):** For senior roles, an interview or presentation to a promotion panel may be required.
4.  **Panel Recommendation:** A cross-functional panel (for senior roles) or direct manager with leadership approval recommends the promotion.
5.  **HR & Leadership Approval:** Final approval is granted by HR and relevant senior leadership.
6.  **Offer & Communication:** The employee is informed of the promotion, new role, and compensation adjustments.

## 10. Compensation, Bonuses & Rewards

ChandraAILabs believes in fair and competitive compensation and rewards employees for their contributions to our success, particularly those driving our AI product innovation.

### 10.1. Annual Salary Increments

*   Annual salary increments are typically awarded based on the employee's Annual Performance Review rating, market competitiveness, and company financial performance.
*   Employees with a rating of '3', '4', or '5' are generally eligible for an increment.
*   Employees with a rating of '1' or '2' may receive no increment or a reduced increment, reflecting the need for performance improvement.

### 10.2. Bonus Structure Tied to AI Product Metrics

ChandraAILabs operates an annual bonus program tied to the achievement of individual, team, and company goals, with a significant emphasis on AI product success.

*   **Eligibility:** All full-time employees, subject to minimum performance rating ('3' or above) and being employed at the time of bonus payout.
*   **Bonus Pool:** Determined by ChandraAILabs' overall financial performance and strategic goals.
*   **Bonus Calculation Components:**
    *   **Individual Performance (e.g., 20-30% weight):** Based on individual OKR achievement and Annual Performance Review rating.
    *   **Team/Department Performance (e.g., 30-40% weight):** Based on the achievement of specific team OKRs, project milestones, and successful delivery of AI features or models.
    *   **Company Performance (e.g., 40-50% weight):** Based on overall company profitability, revenue growth, and key **AI Product Metrics**.

*   **Key AI Product Metrics for Bonus Calculation (Company Component examples):**
    *   **Model Performance Improvement:** Overall reduction in error rates (e.g., RMSE, MAE), increase in accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score across core AI models.
    *   **AI Feature Adoption/Engagement:** Growth in Daily Active Users (DAU), Monthly Active Users (MAU) for AI-powered features, feature utilization rates.
    *   **AI Product Revenue/Impact:** Revenue generated directly or indirectly by new AI products/features, cost savings achieved through AI automation.
    *   **Customer Satisfaction (AI-specific):** NPS (Net Promoter Score) or CSAT related to AI product functionality and reliability.
    *   **Innovation & Research Output:** Number of successful AI prototypes moved to development, publications, patents filed, or significant breakthroughs in AI research applied to products.
    *   **Scalability & Efficiency:** Improvements in AI model inference latency, throughput, or reduction in computational costs.

*   **Discretionary Rewards:** Managers may award ad-hoc recognition for exceptional contributions throughout the year, especially for outstanding innovation or problem-solving in AI/ML projects.

## 11. Roles and Responsibilities

*   **Employees:** Actively participate in goal setting, take ownership of their performance and development, seek feedback, and engage constructively in review discussions.
*   **Managers:** Set clear OKRs, provide regular feedback and coaching, conduct fair and objective performance reviews, support employee development, and address performance issues promptly.
*   **Human Resources (HR):** Develop, implement, and maintain this policy; provide training and guidance to managers and employees; facilitate calibration; ensure fairness, consistency, and compliance.
*   **Senior Leadership:** Champion the performance management culture, set strategic company OKRs, ensure resource allocation for development, and provide ultimate oversight.

## 12. Confidentiality

All performance-related discussions, documents, and ratings are confidential. Managers and employees are expected to maintain the privacy of this information. Access to performance records is restricted to the employee, their direct manager, relevant leadership, and HR.

## 13. Policy Review and Updates

This policy will be reviewed annually by the HR department in consultation with senior leadership to ensure its effectiveness, relevance, and alignment with ChandraAILabs' evolving business needs and legal requirements. Any updates will be communicated to all employees.
