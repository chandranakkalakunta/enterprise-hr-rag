# ChandraAILabs Employee Onboarding Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 26, 2023
**Review Date:** October 26, 2024
**Prepared By:** HR Department, ChandraAILabs

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to ChandraAILabs! We are thrilled to have you join our team of innovators and pioneers in the AI/ML product space. This Onboarding Policy is designed to ensure a smooth, comprehensive, and engaging transition for all new employees into the ChandraAILabs family. Our goal is to equip you with the knowledge, tools, and support needed to quickly become a productive and valued member of our dynamic team, contributing to cutting-edge AI/ML solutions.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:
*   Standardize the onboarding process across ChandraAILabs.
*   Provide a clear roadmap for new hires from offer acceptance through their first six months.
*   Ensure timely completion of all administrative, IT, and compliance requirements.
*   Facilitate cultural and social integration into the ChandraAILabs environment.
*   Accelerate the new hire's understanding of their role, team, company vision, and our AI/ML product landscape.
*   Foster a sense of belonging and engagement from day one.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees joining ChandraAILabs in Hyderabad, India, regardless of their position or department.

## 4. Policy Statement

ChandraAILabs is committed to providing a structured and supportive onboarding experience that enables every new employee to thrive. We believe that a robust onboarding process is crucial for employee retention, productivity, and overall job satisfaction. The Human Resources (HR) department, in collaboration with Hiring Managers, IT, and designated Buddies, will ensure that all aspects of this policy are diligently implemented.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

*   **HR Department:** Overall owner of the onboarding process, policy administration, pre-joining communication, document collection, compliance, general orientation, and ongoing support.
*   **Hiring Manager:** Responsible for team integration, defining role expectations, assigning tasks, conducting regular check-ins, performance management during probation, and facilitating role-specific training.
*   **IT Department:** Ensures timely setup of hardware, software, network access, and necessary AI/ML tool access.
*   **Buddy:** Provides informal support, cultural guidance, introduces the new hire to colleagues, answers day-to-day questions, and helps them navigate the company environment.
*   **New Hire:** Actively participates in all onboarding activities, completes required tasks and training, asks questions, seeks feedback, and integrates into the team.

## 6. Onboarding Phases

The onboarding process at ChandraAILabs is structured into several phases to ensure a holistic integration experience.

### Phase 1: Pre-Joining (Offer Acceptance to Day 0)

This phase focuses on ensuring the new hire feels welcomed and prepared before their first day.

#### 6.1. HR Responsibilities:
*   **Offer Letter & Background Verification:** Extend a formal offer letter and initiate background checks (if applicable).
*   **Welcome Email/Call:** Send a personalized welcome email outlining first-day logistics, what to bring, and who to contact.
*   **Pre-joining Checklist:** Share the pre-joining checklist with the new hire (refer to Section 6.1.1).
*   **Document Collection:** Request submission of necessary KYC and employment documents.
*   **System Access Request:** Initiate IT requests for hardware, software, email, and system access.
*   **Buddy Assignment:** Assign a buddy and facilitate initial contact (refer to Section 6.1.2).
*   **Workspace Preparation:** Coordinate with the Hiring Manager and IT to ensure a ready workspace.

#### 6.1.1. Pre-Joining Checklist for New Hires:
*   **Acknowledge Offer Letter:** Sign and return the offer letter.
*   **Complete Background Verification Forms:** Provide necessary details and consent.
*   **Submit Required Documents (Scanned Copies initially):**
    *   Proof of Identity (PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport)
    *   Proof of Address (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Utility Bill)
    *   Proof of Education (Highest Degree Certificate & Marksheets)
    *   Proof of Previous Employment (Relieving Letter(s), Experience Certificate(s), Last 3 months' Payslips)
    *   Bank Account Details (Canceled Cheque or Bank Statement)
    *   Passport-sized Photographs (2-4 copies)
    *   UAN (Universal Account Number) for PF (if applicable)
    *   ESIC Pehchan Card/Number (if applicable)
*   **Review Welcome Kit:** Read through the welcome email and any attached introductory materials.
*   **Connect with Buddy:** Initiate contact with the assigned buddy.
*   **Plan Commute:** Familiarize yourself with the office location and commute options to Hyderabad Knowledge City (Gachibowli/Hitec City area).

#### 6.1.2. Buddy System Initialization:
*   A 'Buddy' will be assigned to each new hire typically 1-2 weeks before their start date.
*   The Buddy will reach out to introduce themselves and offer support.
*   The Buddy's role is to be a friendly face, answer informal questions, help navigate office culture, and introduce the new hire to colleagues and facilities.

### Phase 2: First Day Process (Day 1)

The first day is critical for setting a positive tone.

#### 6.2. Arrival & Welcome:
*   **Warm Welcome:** Greeted by HR and/or Hiring Manager.
*   **HR Formalities:**
    *   Physical submission and verification of original KYC and employment documents.
    *   Completion of HR forms (PF, ESIC, Gratuity, Nomination Forms, Employee Information Form).
    *   Overview of company policies (HR, Code of Conduct, Leave Policy, etc.).
    *   Introduction to employee benefits (health insurance, provident fund, gratuity).
*   **IT Setup & Orientation (Refer to Section 6.2.1):**
    *   Provision of laptop/workstation.
    *   Login credentials for primary systems (email, internal network).
    *   Basic IT security briefing.
*   **Team Introduction:** Introduced to the immediate team and other key colleagues by the Hiring Manager.
*   **Office Tour:** Guided tour of the office facilities, including cafeteria, restrooms, meeting rooms, and emergency exits.
*   **Buddy Introduction:** Formal introduction to the assigned Buddy.
*   **First Day Lunch:** Encourage lunch with the team or Buddy.
*   **Initial Task/Project Briefing:** The Hiring Manager provides an overview of immediate tasks and initial project context.

#### 6.2.1. IT Setup & Initial AI Tools Onboarding:
*   **Hardware Provision:**
    *   Assigned Laptop/Workstation (high-performance machines for AI/ML roles).
    *   Monitors, keyboard, mouse.
    *   Headset for communication.
*   **Software & Access:**
    *   Operating System (Windows/Linux/macOS as per role requirement).
    *   Email account setup (Google Workspace/Microsoft 365).
    *   Collaboration Tools (Slack/Microsoft Teams, Confluence, Jira).
    *   Code Repository Access (GitLab/GitHub/Bitbucket).
    *   VPN setup for remote access (if applicable).
    *   **Initial AI Tools Access:**
        *   Access to internal development environment.
        *   Basic credentials for cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP) if required for the role.
        *   Introduction to internal ML platform dashboard (e.g., MLflow, Kubeflow, custom platforms).
        *   Access to internal data storage/lakes with relevant security protocols.

### Phase 3: First Week (Day 2 - Day 5)

This phase focuses on deeper integration and initial learning.

*   **Manager 1:1 Meetings:** Regular check-ins with the Hiring Manager to discuss progress, clarify expectations, and address any questions.
*   **Team Meetings:** Participation in team stand-ups and project meetings.
*   **ChandraAILabs Culture & Values:** Overview of company culture, mission, vision, and core values.
*   **Initial Training:**
    *   **General:** Communication guidelines, internal directory, expense reporting, leave application system.
    *   **Role-Specific:** Deeper dive into project specifics, codebases, and team workflows.
*   **Buddy Check-ins:** Informal meetings with the Buddy to discuss initial experiences and challenges.
*   **HR Follow-up:** HR may conduct a brief check-in to ensure smooth transition.

### Phase 4: First Month (Weeks 2 - 4)

This phase emphasizes active learning, contribution, and continuous support.

*   **Training Schedule (General & AI/ML Specific):**
    *   **General Company Training:**
        *   Data Privacy & Security Policies.
        *   Ethical Guidelines (especially relevant for AI/ML).
        *   Information Security Best Practices.
        *   Understanding of ChandraAILabs' business model and product portfolio.
    *   **AI/ML Tools & Platform Onboarding (Deeper Dive):**
        *   Hands-on sessions with specific internal AI/ML platforms and tools.
        *   Deep dive into MLOps pipelines and workflows.
        *   Access and training on GPU cluster management/resource allocation.
        *   Data governance policies and secure data access for AI/ML projects.
        *   Introduction to internal model registries, experiment tracking, and deployment tools.
        *   Overview of the tech stack: Python, TensorFlow/PyTorch, Docker, Kubernetes, Apache Spark, etc.
        *   Access to internal knowledge base, research papers, and technical documentation specific to ChandraAILabs' AI/ML endeavors.
    *   **Mentorship:** Potentially assigned a mentor for technical guidance.
*   **Goal Setting:** Collaborative setting of SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals for the probation period with the Hiring Manager.
*   **Project Immersion:** Active contribution to projects, starting with smaller tasks.
*   **Cross-functional Introductions:** Introduction to key stakeholders in other departments (e.g., Product Management, Data Engineering, Research).

### Phase 5: Probation Period & Performance Management (Months 2 - 6)

All new employees at ChandraAILabs undergo a **six (6) months probation period**. This period allows both the employee and the company to assess suitability for the role and the company culture.

*   **Regular Check-ins:** Weekly/Bi-weekly 1:1 meetings with the Hiring Manager to review progress, provide feedback, and offer support.
*   **Mid-Probation Review (Month 3):**
    *   Formal performance review conducted by the Hiring Manager with HR involvement.
    *   Review of initial goals, accomplishments, areas for development, and feedback.
    *   Opportunity for the employee to provide feedback on their onboarding experience.
*   **Learning & Development:** Identification of further training needs, internal workshops, or external courses relevant to career growth and role enhancement.
*   **Feedback Mechanisms:** Encouragement of continuous feedback loops between the new hire, manager, and team.

### Phase 6: Probation Completion (End of Month 6)

Towards the end of the 6-month probation period, a comprehensive review will be conducted.

*   **Final Probation Review:**
    *   The Hiring Manager will conduct a final performance review, assessing the new hire's overall performance, adherence to company policies, cultural fit, and contribution against set goals.
    *   HR will be involved in the review process to ensure fairness and consistency.
*   **Recommendation:** Based on the review, the Hiring Manager will recommend one of the following to HR:
    *   **Confirmation of Employment:** If performance meets or exceeds expectations.
    *   **Extension of Probation:** If additional time is needed to assess performance or address specific development areas (maximum 3 months extension).
    *   **Separation of Employment:** If the employee's performance or fit is not deemed suitable for ChandraAILabs.
*   **Formal Communication:** The HR Department will issue a formal letter confirming the outcome of the probation period. Upon successful completion, the employee will be considered a permanent employee of ChandraAILabs.
