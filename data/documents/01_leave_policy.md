## 1. Introduction

TechCorp India is committed to fostering a supportive and productive work environment. This Employee Leave Policy outlines the various types of leaves available to our employees, their eligibility criteria, and the procedures for availing them. This policy is designed to ensure fairness, transparency, and compliance with applicable Indian labour laws.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:
*   Provide a clear framework for managing employee leave entitlements.
*   Ensure that employees have adequate time for rest, personal commitments, and recuperation from illness.
*   Maintain operational efficiency by establishing a clear leave application and approval process.
*   Comply with statutory requirements and best industry practices in India.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to all regular, full-time employees of TechCorp India, unless otherwise specified for certain leave types. Contractual, temporary, or part-time employees may be subject to different leave policies as outlined in their respective employment contracts or agreements.

## 4. General Principles

*   All leaves are subject to prior approval by the employee's immediate manager and the HR department, except in cases of genuine emergencies.
*   Leave cannot be claimed as a matter of right. Operational requirements and workload may necessitate the deferment of leave, in which case the employee will be informed well in advance.
*   Employees are responsible for planning their leaves to minimize disruption to work.
*   Misrepresentation or falsification of information for availing leave may lead to disciplinary action, including termination of employment.
*   Leave entitlements are generally calculated on a calendar year basis (January 1st to December 31st).

## 5. Leave Categories

### 5.1 Privilege Leave (PL) / Annual Leave

*   **Entitlement:** 21 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Leave will be accrued on a pro-rata basis at the rate of 1.75 days per completed month of service. Accrual begins from the date of joining, but leave can typically only be availed after successful completion of the probation period.
*   **Eligibility:** All regular employees after successful completion of their probation period. For new joiners, PL will be credited pro-rata for the remaining part of the calendar year.
*   **Purpose:** For rest, rejuvenation, and personal matters requiring extended time off.
*   **Application Process:** Must be applied for at least **15 working days in advance** through the designated HR portal. For leave requests of 5 days or more, a notice period of 1 month is highly recommended.
*   **Minimum/Maximum Usage:** PL can typically be availed for a minimum of 1 day. Management may restrict the maximum continuous leave period based on business needs.
*   **Clubbing with other leaves:** PL can generally be combined with other leave types (e.g., Casual Leave) with prior approval.
*   **Carry Forward:** Please refer to [Section 7.1](#71-privilege-leave-pl).

### 5.2 Casual Leave (CL)

*   **Entitlement:** 7 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Generally credited in full at the beginning of the calendar year (January 1st). For new joiners, CL will be credited pro-rata for the remaining part of the calendar year, calculated at 0.58 days per month.
*   **Eligibility:** All regular employees after successful completion of their probation period.
*   **Purpose:** For unforeseen personal matters, short-duration emergencies, or urgent personal work that cannot be planned in advance.
*   **Application Process:** Should be applied for through the designated HR portal as soon as the need arises. For planned casual leave, a notice of at least **3 working days** is recommended. In genuine emergencies, employees must inform their manager immediately via phone/email and submit the formal application upon return.
*   **Usage:** CL is typically granted for short durations (1-2 days). Continuous CL for more than 3 days may require detailed justification and management discretion.
*   **Carry Forward:** CL **cannot be carried forward** to the next calendar year and will lapse on December 31st of each year.
*   **Encashment:** CL is not encashable.

### 5.3 Sick Leave (SL)

*   **Entitlement:** 10 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Generally credited in full at the beginning of the calendar year (January 1st). For new joiners, SL will be credited pro-rata for the remaining part of the calendar year, calculated at 0.83 days per month.
*   **Eligibility:** All regular employees after successful completion of their probation period.
*   **Purpose:** For an employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments.
*   **Application Process:** Employees must inform their immediate manager and HR department at the earliest possible opportunity (preferably before the start of their shift) via phone/email, followed by a formal application through the HR portal.
*   **Medical Certificate:**
    *   For sick leave of **2 consecutive days or more**, a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory upon return to work.
    *   TechCorp India reserves the right to request a medical certificate for any duration of sick leave, or to refer an employee to a company-designated doctor for assessment, especially in cases of frequent or prolonged sick leave.
*   **Carry Forward:** SL **cannot be carried forward** to the next calendar year and will lapse on December 31st of each year.
*   **Encashment:** SL is not encashable.

### 5.4 Maternity Leave

*   **Entitlement:** 26 weeks (182 calendar days) for up to two surviving children.
*   **Eligibility:** Female employees who have worked at TechCorp India for at least 80 days in the 12 months immediately preceding the date of her expected delivery. This is as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended in 2017).
*   **Usage:**
    *   Up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected date of delivery.
    *   The remaining leave must be taken after delivery.
    *   For female employees having two or more surviving children, the entitlement is 12 weeks (no more than 6 weeks before expected delivery).
*   **Adoption Leave:** Female employees who legally adopt a child below the age of three months shall be entitled to 12 weeks of maternity leave from the date the child is handed over.
*   **Surrogacy Leave:** Commissioning mothers (who use a surrogate to bear a child) shall be entitled to 12 weeks of maternity leave from the date the child is handed over.
*   **Miscarriage/Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP):** Employees who undergo a miscarriage or MTP are entitled to 6 weeks of leave with pay immediately following the date of miscarriage/MTP.
*   **Complications:** In case of illness arising out of pregnancy, delivery, premature birth of a child, or miscarriage, an additional one month of leave with wages may be granted by the employer upon submission of proof.
*   **Benefits:** Full wages during the maternity leave period.
*   **Application Process:** Employees must notify HR and their immediate manager in writing at least **2 months in advance** of their expected due date, providing necessary medical documentation. A formal application must be submitted through the HR portal.

### 5.5 Paternity Leave

*   **Entitlement:** 5 working days.
*   **Eligibility:** Male employees on the birth of their child (biological or legally adopted).
*   **Purpose:** To support the spouse during childbirth and bond with the newborn or newly adopted child.
*   **Usage:** Can be taken anytime within 3 months before the expected date of delivery/adoption or within 6 months after the actual date of delivery/adoption. It can be availed continuously or split into two periods with prior approval.
*   **Application Process:** Must be applied for at least **7 working days in advance** through the HR portal, with relevant supporting documents (e.g., doctor's certificate, adoption papers) to be submitted to HR.
*   **Carry Forward/Encashment:** Paternity Leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### 5.6 Public Holidays

*   **Entitlement:** TechCorp India observes a list of gazetted public holidays each calendar year as declared by the relevant government authorities.
*   **List:** An annual list of declared public holidays will be circulated to all employees by the HR department at the beginning of each calendar year.
*   **Working on Public Holidays:** In exceptional circumstances, if an employee is required to work on a declared public holiday due to critical business needs, they will be compensated either with an equivalent number of Compensatory Off (COFF) days or overtime pay, as per company policy and management discretion. Prior approval from the respective Department Head and HR is mandatory for working on a public holiday.

### 5.7 Other Leave Categories

TechCorp India acknowledges that employees may require time off for other significant life events. While not explicitly defined with fixed entitlements, these leaves may be granted on a case-by-case basis at the discretion of management and HR.

*   **Bereavement Leave:** In the unfortunate event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), employees may be granted up to 3 working days of paid leave. Additional unpaid leave may be considered based on circumstances.
*   **Jury Duty/Civic Duty Leave:** If required by law to perform jury duty or other civic duties, employees must inform HR immediately. Paid leave may be granted for the period required, subject to submission of official documentation.
*   **Unpaid Leave/Leave Without Pay (LWP):** In exceptional circumstances, and after exhausting all other available paid leaves, employees may request LWP. Such requests require strong justification and are subject to management and HR approval, considering business needs. LWP impacts service benefits (e.g., gratuity, provident fund contributions) as per statutory regulations.

## 6. Leave Application Process

1.  **Request Submission:** All leave requests must be submitted through the designated HR Management System (HRMS) portal.
2.  **Manager Approval:** The request will be routed to the employee's immediate manager for review and approval. Managers will consider team schedules, project deadlines, and operational requirements before approving or rejecting a leave request.
3.  **HR Approval:** For certain leave types (e.g., Maternity, Paternity, extended PL), HR approval may also be required.
4.  **Notification:** Employees will be notified of the approval or rejection of their leave request via the HRMS portal and/or email.
5.  **Documentation:** For leaves requiring documentation (e.g., medical certificates for Sick Leave, birth certificates for Paternity Leave), employees must upload or submit these documents to HR as required by the policy.
6.  **Emergency Leave:** In case of an emergency that prevents prior application, employees must inform their manager immediately (via phone/text/email) and submit a formal leave application through the HRMS portal as soon as possible, ideally on the first day of absence or upon return to work.

## 7. Leave Accrual and Carry Forward Rules

### 7.1 Privilege Leave (PL)

*   **Accrual:** 1.75 days per completed month of service. Accrued leave will be credited at the end of each month.
*   **Maximum Carry Forward:** A maximum of **45 days** of accrued Privilege Leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year.
*   **Lapsing:** Any PL balance exceeding 45 days at the end of the calendar year (December 31st) will automatically lapse and cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### 7.2 Casual Leave (CL)

*   **Accrual:** Full entitlement credited at the beginning of the calendar year, pro-rata for new joiners.
*   **Carry Forward:** CL cannot be carried forward and will lapse on December 31st of each year.

### 7.3 Sick Leave (SL)

*   **Accrual:** Full entitlement credited at the beginning of the calendar year, pro-rata for new joiners.
*   **Carry Forward:** SL cannot be carried forward and will lapse on December 31st of each year.

## 8. Leave Encashment

*   **Privilege Leave (PL):**
    *   Employees are not permitted to encash active PL balance during employment, except upon separation from the company.
    *   Upon resignation, retirement, or termination, any accumulated and unutilized Privilege Leave (up to the maximum carry-forward limit) will be encashed at the employee's last drawn basic salary, provided the employee has served the applicable notice period or notice pay has been recovered.
*   **Casual Leave (CL) & Sick Leave (SL):** CL and SL are not encashable under any circumstances, including separation from the company.

## 9. Leave During Probation Period

*   During the probation period, employees are generally **not entitled to paid Privilege Leave**.
*   **Casual Leave (CL)** and **Sick Leave (SL)** may be availed on a pro-rata basis, but generally unpaid or adjusted against future accruals if the probation is successfully completed. Each case will be reviewed by HR and the manager.
*   In case of extended absence during probation, the probation period may be extended accordingly.
*   Maternity Leave entitlement is as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, which requires 80 days of service in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.

## 10. Unauthorised Absence

*   Any absence from work without prior approval or proper notification, or overstaying an approved leave without valid reason and approval, will be considered unauthorised absence.
*   Unauthorised absence will result in loss of pay for the period of absence and may lead to disciplinary action, including a warning, suspension, or termination of employment, depending on the duration and frequency of such absence.
*   If an employee is absent for **3 consecutive working days** without informing the company, it will be presumed that the employee has abandoned their employment, and appropriate action will be initiated as per company policy.

## 11. Leave on Termination of Employment

*   Upon cessation of employment (resignation, retirement, termination), any unutilized Privilege Leave balance (up to the maximum carry-forward limit) will be encashed as per [Section 8](#8-leave-encashment).
*   Casual Leave and Sick Leave balances will lapse immediately upon the last day of employment and are not encashable.
