# Employee Leave Policy - ChandraAILabs

## 1. Introduction and Purpose

ChandraAILabs is committed to fostering a supportive and productive work environment that prioritizes employee well-being and work-life balance. This Employee Leave Policy outlines the various types of leave available to our permanent employees, the eligibility criteria, application procedures, and general guidelines. The policy aims to provide clarity, ensure fairness, comply with relevant labor laws applicable in Hyderabad, India, and enable the smooth functioning of business operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of ChandraAILabs based in Hyderabad, India, who are on the company's payroll. Contractual employees, interns, and temporary staff may have separate leave arrangements as per their specific terms of engagement.

## 3. General Principles

*   All leave requests must be submitted and approved in advance, except in cases of genuine emergencies.
*   Leave is not an absolute right and will be granted at the discretion of the Management, considering business exigencies and team workload.
*   Employees are expected to plan their leaves to minimize disruption to work.
*   Abuse of leave policy may lead to disciplinary action.
*   This policy is subject to change at the discretion of ChandraAILabs management and will be communicated to all employees.
*   All leaves are calculated on a calendar year basis (January 1st to December 31st), unless otherwise specified.

## 4. Types of Leave

ChandraAILabs provides the following types of paid leave:

### 4.1. Annual Leave (AL) / Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave (PL)

*   **Entitlement:** 21 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Annual Leave accrues on a pro-rata basis, typically at 1.75 days per month, starting from the employee's date of joining or confirmation, whichever is specified in the employment contract.
*   **Eligibility:** Employees are eligible to avail Annual Leave only upon completion of their probation period and confirmation of employment. Leave accrued during probation may be utilized after confirmation.
*   **Purpose:** To provide employees with an opportunity for rest, recreation, and personal matters.
*   **Minimum/Maximum:** A minimum of 1 day and a maximum of 15 consecutive days of Annual Leave can generally be taken at one time. Any request for longer duration requires special approval from HR and the reporting manager.
*   **Notice Period:** A minimum of 15-30 days prior notice is required for Annual Leave requests to allow for proper work planning and handover.

### 4.2. Sick Leave (SL)

*   **Entitlement:** 10 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Sick Leave is credited at the beginning of the calendar year or on a pro-rata basis for employees joining mid-year.
*   **Purpose:** To cover periods of illness, injury, or medical appointments that prevent an employee from performing their duties.
*   **Medical Certificate:** For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory. ChandraAILabs reserves the right to request a medical certificate for shorter periods as well.
*   **Notification:** Employees must notify their manager and HR as soon as possible (preferably within the first hour of work) on the first day of absence due to sickness.
*   **Non-Encashable/Non-Carry Forward:** Sick Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and cannot be encashed. Any unused Sick Leave will lapse at the end of the calendar year.

### 4.3. Casual Leave (CL)

*   **Entitlement:** 7 working days per calendar year.
*   **Accrual:** Casual Leave is credited at the beginning of the calendar year or on a pro-rata basis for employees joining mid-year.
*   **Purpose:** To address unforeseen personal matters, urgent family commitments, or short breaks.
*   **Prior Approval:** Prior approval from the reporting manager is mandatory for availing Casual Leave, even for half-day requests. While short notice may be acceptable in genuine emergencies, employees are encouraged to provide as much notice as possible (ideally 1-2 days).
*   **Maximum:** Generally, not more than 3 consecutive days of Casual Leave can be taken at a time.
*   **Non-Encashable/Non-Carry Forward:** Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and cannot be encashed. Any unused Casual Leave will lapse at the end of the calendar year.

### 4.4. Maternity Leave (ML)

*   **Entitlement:** 26 weeks (182 calendar days) for up to two surviving children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended).
*   **Eligibility:** Female employees who have worked for ChandraAILabs for at least 80 days in the 12 months immediately preceding the date of her expected delivery.
*   **Distribution:**
    *   Up to 8 weeks of leave can be taken before the expected date of delivery.
    *   The remaining leave must be taken after delivery.
*   **Special Provisions:**
    *   **Adoption:** For commissioning mothers or mothers adopting a child below three months of age, 12 weeks of leave from the date the child is handed over.
    *   **Surrogacy:** A commissioning mother (who uses her egg to create an embryo implanted in another woman) is entitled to 12 weeks of leave from the date the child is handed over.
    *   **Miscarriage/Medical Termination of Pregnancy:** 6 weeks of leave from the date of miscarriage/MTP.
    *   **Tubectomy Operation:** 2 weeks of leave from the date of the operation.
    *   **Illness arising out of Pregnancy:** An additional one month of leave (beyond 26 weeks) may be granted on medical grounds, subject to approval from HR and supporting medical documentation.
*   **Notification:** Employees must provide written notification to HR and their manager regarding their pregnancy and intention to avail maternity leave, along with the expected date of delivery, at least 4-6 weeks in advance. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner confirming the pregnancy and expected delivery date is required.

### 4.5. Paternity Leave (PL)

*   **Entitlement:** 5 working days per calendar year.
*   **Eligibility:** All permanent male employees.
*   **Purpose:** To enable fathers to support their partners during childbirth or adoption and to bond with the newborn/adopted child.
*   **Timing:** Paternity Leave can be availed within 30 days before or up to 60 days after the date of childbirth or adoption.
*   **Notification:** Employees must provide written notification to HR and their manager at least 15 days in advance, wherever possible, along with proof of childbirth/adoption (e.g., birth certificate, adoption papers).
*   **Non-Encashable/Non-Carry Forward:** Paternity Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and cannot be encashed.

### 4.6. Public Holidays

*   **Entitlement:** ChandraAILabs observes all mandatory National Holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti).
*   **Additional Holidays:** In addition to National Holidays, ChandraAILabs will declare a list of approximately 7-9 regional/festival holidays at the beginning of each calendar year, based on the prevalent practices in Hyderabad and Telangana.
*   **Working on a Public Holiday:** In exceptional cases where an employee is required to work on a declared Public Holiday, compensatory off (COFF) or additional remuneration will be provided as per company policy and applicable labor laws.

## 5. Leave Application and Approval Process

1.  **Submission:** All leave requests (except for immediate Sick Leave notification) must be submitted through the designated HR portal/system.
2.  **Information:** The leave application must specify the type of leave, start date, end date, total number of days, reason for leave, and contact details during leave.
3.  **Manager Approval:** The request will be routed to the employee's reporting manager for review and approval. Managers will consider team workload, project deadlines, and overall business needs before approving or rejecting a leave request.
4.  **HR Review:** Upon manager's approval, the request may be routed to HR for final review and record-keeping, especially for longer leaves or special cases.
5.  **Confirmation:** Employees will receive an email or system notification confirming the approval or rejection of their leave request.
6.  **Emergency Leave:** In case of a sudden illness or emergency preventing an employee from coming to work, they must immediately inform their reporting manager and HR (via phone call or urgent message) on the first day of absence. A formal leave application must be submitted through the HR portal as soon as practicable.
7.  **Handover:** Employees proceeding on leave are responsible for ensuring proper handover of their duties and responsibilities to a designated colleague, and for setting appropriate out-of-office replies.

## 6. Leave Carry Forward and Encashment Rules

### 6.1. Annual Leave (AL) / Earned Leave (EL)

*   **Carry Forward:** A maximum of 30 days of unused Annual Leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any Annual Leave exceeding this limit at the end of the year will lapse.
*   **Encashment:** Encashment of Annual Leave is generally not permitted during employment. However, accumulated Annual Leave (up to the maximum permissible limit as per law and company policy) will be encashed at the time of resignation, superannuation, or termination, provided the employee has completed the required service period.

### 6.2. Sick Leave (SL), Casual Leave (CL), Paternity Leave (PL)

*   These leave types cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
*   They cannot be encashed.
*   Any unused Sick Leave, Casual Leave, or Paternity Leave will lapse at the end of the calendar year.

### 6.3. Maternity Leave (ML)

*   Maternity Leave is a specific-purpose leave and does not involve carry-forward or encashment.

## 7. Leave During Probation

*   During the probation period, employees are generally not eligible for paid Annual Leave.
*   They may be granted paid Sick Leave and Casual Leave on a pro-rata basis, subject to management approval, for genuine reasons.
*   Any leave taken during probation, if unpaid, will extend the probation period accordingly.

## 8. Leave Without Pay (LWP) / Unpaid Leave

*   In exceptional circumstances, after exhausting all available paid leave, an employee may request Leave Without Pay (LWP).
*   LWP requests must be submitted in advance with a valid reason and require approval from the reporting manager and HR.
*   LWP may impact an employee's service benefits, such as gratuity, provident fund contributions, and appraisal cycles, as per company policy and statutory regulations.

## 9. Leave for Resigning Employees

*   Employees serving their notice period are expected to utilize any remaining accrued Annual Leave only with prior approval from their manager and HR, considering the need for proper handover during the notice period.
*   Any balance of accrued and unutilized Annual Leave will be encashed at the time of full and final settlement, subject to company policy and labor laws.
*   Sick Leave and Casual Leave will not be encashed and will lapse upon the last working day.

## 10. Policy Review

ChandraAILabs reserves the right to amend, modify, or withdraw any part of this policy at any time, based on business needs, changes in statutory regulations, or internal reviews. Any changes will be communicated to all employees.
